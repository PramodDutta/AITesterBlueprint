# AI Framework Fundamentals

## Goal

Understand where AI actually helps in test framework work.

## Key Ideas

- AI is strong at scaffolding repetitive structures.
- AI is useful for generating boilerplate, DTOs, page objects, helper methods, and starter assertions.
- AI is weak when framework conventions are vague or undocumented.
- AI output must be reviewed for maintainability, selector quality, waits, naming, and architecture consistency.

## QA Takeaway

Use AI as a framework accelerator, not as the framework architect of record.
